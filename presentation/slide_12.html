Phased Development Roadmap

Clear engineering milestones to advance from a working prototype to an enterprise appliance.

STAGE 1
Current Working Prototype (Completed)
Fast reverse proxy, sub-millisecond AST parser, 28-rule CRS suite, Slider CAPTCHA, and centralized dashboard.
STAGE 2
Months 1–3 @ BiTec: Traffic Baselining & Rate Limiting
Implementing sliding-window rate limiting, client connection frequency tracking, and behavioral scanning baselining.
STAGE 3
Months 4–6 @ BiTec: Zero-Day Anomaly Detection
Adding lightweight local classification models to recognize polymorphic injection mutations and automated parameter fuzzing.
STAGE 4
Months 7–9 @ BiTec: Plain-Language AI Summaries
Automated incident reporting that converts raw request dumps and regex logs into plain-language incident explanations and patch advice.
