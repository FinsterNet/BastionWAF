The Target Market: The "Missing Middle"

Bastion protects the critical tier of institutions that handle real user data locally but cannot access foreign firewalls.

Sector 01
Campus Networks & Departmental Services
BiT student portals, grading platforms, and faculty research tools facing 24/7 automated network scanning without dedicated security staff or enterprise security operations centers.
Sector 02
Regional SMEs & Software Houses
Local tech startups, e-commerce shops, and private clinics processing customer transactions entirely in Ethiopian Birr (ETB) and needing a turnkey, zero-headache perimeter firewall.
Sector 03
Public Regional Platforms
Municipal and educational bureaus legally or policy-bound to keep all traffic data, inspection telemetry, and audit logs strictly on physical, on-premises local servers within national borders.
