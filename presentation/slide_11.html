Tiered Subscriptions (SaaS)

Channel 03
Domestic Partnerships
•Hosting Provider Add-on (300 – 500 ETB / domain / mo): Wholesale integration with domestic hosting providers during checkout.
•Software Agency Bundling: Regional software houses pre-installing Bastion as the baseline security layer on custom client portals.
