Incubation Ask: Why BiTec?

Four essential incubator resources requested to transition Bastion into production.

1
Workstation & Continuous Electrical Power
Dedicated workstation access with dependable electrical power and network connectivity to conduct sustained 48-hour traffic stress tests, fuzzing pipelines, and proxy benchmarking.
2
Isolated Security Testing Subnet
A closed virtual lab environment to safely execute attack tools (SQLMap, Nuclei) against test targets through Bastion without risking campus network stability.
3
Faculty Technical Mentorship
Advisory guidance from BiT computing professors on socket pooling, high-throughput concurrency, and statistical anomaly modeling.
4
Campus Staging Pilot Authorization
Permission to test Bastion in Passive Monitor (Bypass) Mode in front of an internal departmental test service to validate real-world reliability with zero downtime risk.
